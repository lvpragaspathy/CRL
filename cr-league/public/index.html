How ratings work
  • Ratings use Glicko-2. Everyone starts at 1500 with a rating deviation (RD) of 350. RD is the ± next to a rating: how sure the system is. It shrinks as you play and grows while you're away.
  • Beating someone rated higher earns more, and results move uncertain ratings (new players, people coming back) faster than settled ones. Draws split the points; tiebreaker wins count as wins.
  • Crowns matter. Against an even opponent a 2-crown win moves ratings 1.15× as much as a 1-crown win, and a 3-crown win 1.3×. The bonus grows when the winner was the underdog and shrinks when a big favorite stomps a weaker player. The loser drops by the same multiple.
  • Win chances come from both players' ratings and RDs, so two uncertain ratings pull the odds toward 50/50. An Upset is a win the system gave under 35%.
  • Seasons run by calendar month. On the 1st, every rating moves halfway back to 1500 and RD goes up to at least 150, so the first few games of a season are placements. The top rating at month's end with 5+ battles is crowned season champion.
  • Decks come straight from the battle log for auto-imported matches. Card stats count only battles with deck data.
  • Tiers: Bronze under 1450, Silver 1450+, Gold 1550+, Diamond 1650+, Champion 1750+. Placement shows while RD is above 110.
  • Nothing is stored as a rating: it's recalculated from the full match history, so fixing an old match fixes everything after it.
