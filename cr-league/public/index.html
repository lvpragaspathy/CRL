How ratings work
  • Everyone starts at 1000. Ratings are Elo-style: beating someone rated higher earns more than beating someone rated lower.
  • Your first 10 rated matches are placement games and move your rating faster (K = 40). After that K = 24.
  • Margin matters a little: a 2-crown win is worth 1.15×, a 3-crown win 1.3×. Tiebreaker wins count as normal wins; draws split the points.
  • Ratings are recalculated from the full match history every time, so editing or deleting an old match fixes everything after it.
  • Friendlies between two players with tags on their profiles are imported automatically from the Clash Royale API.
  • Tiers: Bronze under 950, Silver 950+, Gold 1050+, Diamond 1150+, Champion 1250+.
